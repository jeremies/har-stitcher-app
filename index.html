Repair ABR upswitch stalls & synthesize continuous HLS trajectories for har-player

RFC 8216 HLS Zero-Build Client
Why har-player freezes when switching resolutions (e.g., 720p ➔ 1080p): In live recordings, only the segments actively requested by the browser's ABR algorithm exist in the HAR log. When the player engine reaches the switch point on the old variant track, it requests the next segment of that same track, receives an HTTP 404, and triggers fatal fragment retry loops. This tool extracts the actual cross-variant trajectory, injects #EXT-X-DISCONTINUITY & #EXT-X-MAP, and rewrites the master manifest so the player streams seamlessly.
